## Findings that change priorities (unaudited, deepseek-flash/high, read before trusting)
Dossier in `~/hocon-fmt-analysis-2026-10-11/dossier/` (files 00-07; `00-index.md` first). Collector reports
beside it. These came from cheap collectors and are NOT yet audited:
1. **hocon-fmt refuses 483 of 1,257 real `.conf` files (38.4%)** from 24 shallow-cloned repos (18 are
   applications; per-file verdicts in `corpus/cli-verdicts.tsv`). 394 are `LostComment`: sconfig keeps a
   comment only when a field follows on the next line, so a comment before a blank line, at the end of a
   file or before `}` is dropped and we refuse. 284 are the "licence header + blank line" shape (147
   SPDX). Pekko repos sit at 90-94% refused. This is a v0.1.0 usability question (sconfig #643, #646,
   #647 are the upstream side). 70 files (5.6%) already formatted, 704 would change.
2. **The founding claim "other formatters crash on references between files" is not supported by the
   runs.** With real included files present (six cases), Rust `hoconfmt` 0.1.4 and npm `hoconfmt` 1.4.0
   kept every include verbatim and the resolve-checked value was unchanged. What did fail: lightbend
   `ConfigDocument` drops the closing `)` of `include required(...)`/`file(...)`/`classpath(...)`;
   pyhocon and pip `hocon` crash on `include classpath(...)`; pyhocon, pip `hocon` and lightbend's
   renderer inline or drop includes (value same). Our differentiators may therefore lie elsewhere
   (value-safe duplicate simplification and its shadow refusal, one engine on JVM/JS/Native, hooks and
   build plugins) and the site copy must not claim others crash. `03c-include-survival.md`.
3. **Includes are rare in real application code**: 64 files (5.1%) in 10 of 24 repos, nearly all one
   root-level `include "application"` or `include classpath("application.conf")`; no application repo
   uses nested, `file(...)`, `url(...)`, `required(...)` or cross-directory includes (those exist only in
   parser test corpora). `07-feature-frequency.md`.
4. Duplicate keys in one scope: 197 paths in 102 files; 55 erase an earlier value in 39 files (the
   `--fail-on-duplicates` case). sconfig is strictly stricter than lightbend 1.4.5 (66 parse failures,
   all also lightbend failures; lightbend rejects 18 more).
